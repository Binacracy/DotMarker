Privacy Policy – Binneezy Pin Scheduler
Last updated: October 14, 2026

Binneezy Pin Scheduler is an internal tool operated by Binneezy Kids. It
publishes Pins of our own products to our own Pinterest business account.

Information we access: The app connects only to the Binneezy Kids Pinterest
account, using Pinterest's official OAuth authorization. It reads our own
boards and Pins and creates Pins on our boards. It does not access, collect,
or store data about any other Pinterest user.

Storage: Access tokens are stored locally on our own computer and are used
only to post to our account. We do not sell, share, or transfer any data.

Third parties: No data is shared with third parties. Use of Pinterest is
subject to Pinterest's own privacy policy.

Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's
settings under apps.
